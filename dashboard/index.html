            Your details

            Privacy & your data

            What's saved

            Your settings, tasks and lists, top priorities, check-in answers, gym check-offs, your Proverbs reading log and notes, and the daily commercial real estate and world news links.

            Where it's stored

            In this artifact's private data store on claude.ai, under your Claude account. Access rules limit it to you (and anyone you explicitly give edit access). It is not in any public code repository.

            What isn't saved

            • Calendar events. They're read live from Google Calendar each time you open the page.
            • Assistant chat messages. They disappear when you reload.
            • Passwords or Google tokens. claude.ai's connector handles sign-in; this page never sees them.

            Limits

            • The page only updates while it's open. It doesn't run in the background.
            • The daily articles come from a scheduled morning task in Claude, not from this page.
            • Claude can't read the Wall Street Journal, so world headlines come from other established outlets.
            • Notifications are off.

            Delete your data

            Calendar access

            To turn off calendar access for this page, use the artifact's Permissions menu or disconnect Google Calendar in claude.ai Settings → Connectors.
